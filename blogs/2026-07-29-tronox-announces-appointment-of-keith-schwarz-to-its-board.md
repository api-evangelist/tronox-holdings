STAMFORD, Conn., July 29, 2026 /PRNewswire/ — Tronox Holdings plc (NYSE:TROX) (“Tronox” or the “Company”), the world’s leading integrated manufacturer of titanium dioxide pigment, today announced the appointment of Keith Schwarz to the Board of Directors effective immediately. Mr. Schwarz will also serve as a member of the Audit Committee of the Board.
